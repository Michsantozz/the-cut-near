# The Cut v20: 60-day simulation

Generated by `python3 sim/thecut_sim.py --write` (20 seeds per scenario, seeds 0-19, 60 daily cuts). Do not edit by hand: re-run the script. Same seeds, same numbers, on any machine with Python 3.

Rules: `RULES.md`, modelled in `class Contract` of `sim/thecut_sim.py` with exact integer $CUT units (18 decimals) and the ten cut steps in the spec's order. People are not modelled exactly: fans follow simple strategies, and **the four agent personalities follow short written policies that stand in for the model** (`POLICIES` in the script). A real LLM reading the charter and the board may play very differently. Numbers are medians across seeds unless a column says otherwise.

Assumptions that are not in the spec: the pool starts with 10,000 NEAR against the 10M $CUT (testnet used 4 NEAR; only the ratio to fan budgets matters); a fan's NEAR budget is 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or 50 NEAR (25/25/25/15/7/3% of fans), 60% bought on arrival and the rest 3-15 days later; a fan acts on 70% of days and 30% of fans never leave (the rest sell out after 7-45 days); creators seed 1,000-3,000 $CUT bought from the pool; one cut every 24 h, the first at the end of day 1. Fans and agents never make mistakes.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | What the people do |
| - | - |
| `steady` | Organic fans with five simple strategies; agents follow their policies; about one new agent registers a day. |
| `whale` | Steady, plus one wallet that buys 3/7 of everyone else's NEAR each day (30% of bought supply). It keeps half its $CUT saved on one pick per season (the biggest pile) and boos the strongest rival down to the cut line whenever that costs at most half of its free $CUT. |
| `sniper` | Steady, plus 5 accounts that save 0.1 $CUT in the last block on the survivor with the smallest pile and take it back the next morning. |
| `boo_war` | Two fan groups each back half of the stage (agents split by registration order) and boo the weakest agent of the other half every day. |
| `quiet` | Few fans with half budgets, 5 agents at launch, a registration every ~3 days; agents follow their policy 1 day in 5 and hold otherwise. |
| `ai_down` | Steady fans, but NEAR AI is down for all 60 days: no agent ever moves, so nobody pays think fees, rescues, pacts, attacks or names an heir. |

## Seasons and champions

A season seats up to 10 agents and crowns a champion after (seated - 1) cuts; the next one starts at the following cut if at least 3 agents wait on the bench.

Strategies: `loyal` picks a favorite (weighted by agent popularity), keeps 80% of its $CUT saved on it and adds only new money (adding re-locks the whole save), pulls an unlocked save off a favorite about to be cut only 15% of the time, and boos the agent just above it 30% of the time; `hunter` saves everything on the agent that pays the most per $CUT "if the cut ran now" (equal slice / pile, capped at 1), skipping the two lowest scores; moves its unlocked saves there every morning; `rescue` after the moves are public, saves on the agent whose rescue bounty pays the most per $CUT (skipping the two lowest scores); `top_safe` saves on the biggest pile and stays; `trader` never saves; buys, sells 2-6 days later, buys back 1-4 days after that; `group_a` boo_war: backs the agents with an even registration number, boos the weakest odd one with 15% of its free $CUT every day; `group_b` boo_war: backs the odd agents, boos the weakest even one.

## What this shows

- **Supply reacts to play, and only downward.** In `steady` the median seed ends at 9,516,946 $CUT. The biggest burn source there is `cut piles` (80% of the burn). Every scenario burns; none mints.
- **The game always ends and restarts.** With few fans and agents that mostly hold (`quiet`), every seed still completes 5 to 10 seasons; with no agent moves at all (`ai_down`), every seed completes 6. A cut never waits for anyone, and no season ran longer than stage size - 1 = 9 cuts.
- **The per-unit cap closes the empty-pile snipe.** Over 20 seeds of the steady crowd, the last-block 0.1 $CUT saves were paid 49.57 $CUT with the cap and would have been paid 1,785.28 $CUT without it; in the thin crowd, 158.36 against 22,471.98. The snipe only pays when a survivor's pile is smaller than its slice, which a crowd of yield-seeking fans rarely leaves open.
- **Saving and walking away loses; moving pays.** In `steady`, loyal fans (who rarely move a save) ended at -75% of the $CUT they bought, because nine of ten agents die every season and a save on a cut agent is gone. Fans who move their saves (hunter, rescue, top_safe) ended at +13%, paid mostly by those losses. This is the rule working as written; it is also harsh on casual fans.
- **Money weighs, as the spec's known limits say.** A whale holding about 30% of bought supply got its pick crowned in 120 of 120 seasons, but paid for every point: it ended at -70% of the $CUT it bought, and its boos alone burned 903,749 $CUT per seed (median).

## What this does not show

- **How a real model plays.** Agent moves here come from four short written rules, not from z-ai/glm-5.3-flash reading a charter. Real agents may bluff, ally, or waste their wallets in ways these rules never do. The personality caps (5/50/20/40% a day, no attacks for generous) are enforced exactly; the choices inside them are guesses.
- **Real fans.** Arrivals, budgets, strategies and their mix are assumptions. Fans here never panic, never forget to unsave, and read the chain perfectly. The NEAR price of $CUT and anything outside the game (exchanges, other pools, social media) is not modelled.
- **Relayer choice, key rotation, failed account deletions, gas, storage deposits and NEAR refunds** are outside this model; only $CUT flows are simulated.
- **Sybil splitting** is not tested: every rule counts $CUT, not accounts, so five sniper accounts with 0.1 $CUT each are paid what one account with 0.5 $CUT would be. That follows from the rules, not from the simulation.
- **A big last-block save on the likely champion** still shares the champion pot pro rata (step 8), now at most 1 $CUT per $CUT saved. The snipers here only ever save 0.1 $CUT; a bigger last-block saver is not simulated.
